About

What I’m on now, and what I did before.

I’m currently building a feedback layer for policy improvement in embodied / physical AI. The idea is simple: take what happens in rollouts on a real or simulated body, and turn that into better updates for the policy. Before this, I spent most of my time on drones at Xenova Dynamics (detection, tracking, open datasets). I’m an Entrepreneur in Residence at Entrepreneurs First and a student at IIT Madras.
